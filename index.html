Enterprise RAG Knowledge Base

↗

Turns siloed PDF documentation — HR policies, handbooks, SOPs — into a conversational knowledge base that answers with verifiable page citations. A two-node LangGraph workflow handles retrieve → generate; a collection-per-document strategy in ChromaDB gives zero cross-document leakage and instant deletes. JWT and layered RBAC keep knowledge-base admins and employees strictly apart.
